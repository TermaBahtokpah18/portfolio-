About Us

Joterma Learning Center is dedicated to providing transformative education for students of all ages in Margibi County. We focus on academic excellence, critical thinking, and practical skills development. Our aim is to bring education to less privileged childern with in Margibi and all around Liberia. After the devastating 2014–2016 Ebola crisis in Liberia, many communities were left with broken health systems and disrupted education. In response, local educators, parents, and returning diaspora members came together in Margibi County to launch the Joterma Learning Center. The goal: to restore hope through quality education for children who lost years of schooling and stability. The name Joterma symbolizes “unity, healing, and progress”, derived from a blend of local dialect and the founders’ names.
